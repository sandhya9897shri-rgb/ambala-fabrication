TECHNICAL CATALOG

Detailed Fabrication Specialties

Explore our 8 major architectural fabrication categories, manufactured with zero compromise on raw material quality.

1. UPVC Profile Systems

High-density multi-chambered tropicalized UPVC profiles providing maximum acoustic soundproofing, thermal insulation, and UV stability.

  • Up to 40dB Noise Reduction with DGU Glass
  • Multi-Point Galvanized Steel Reinforcement
  • EPDM Double-Gasket Weather Sealing
  • Sliding, Casement, Tilt & Turn Variants

2. Alu Shield Aluminium

Ultra-luxurious system aluminium doors and windows built for high wind loads, massive structural spans, and minimalist architectural sightlines.

  • Thermal Break Polyamide Strip Technology
  • Heavy Duty Lift & Slide Rail Mechanisms
  • Concealed Drainage Systems
  • Anodized & PVDF Premium Finishes

3. Smart Glass Railings

Frameless structural glass balustrades with continuous aluminium base channels or SS 304/316 heavy spigots for uninterrupted panorama.

  • 12mm - 19mm Toughened & Laminated Glass
  • Heavy Aluminium Continuous Shoe Channel
  • SS 304 Grade Handrails (Round & Slot)
  • Tested to resist 1.5kN/m Lateral Wind Load

4. ACP & HPL Facade Cladding

Exterior elevation cladding utilizing fire-retardant Aluminium Composite Panels (ACP) and natural wood texture High-Pressure Laminates (HPL).

  • 4mm Fire-Retardant Mineral Core ACP
  • Exterior Grade Weatherproof HPL
  • Heavy GI Sub-Framing & Silicone Sealing
  • Wooden, Metallic, and Solid Color Options

5. Smart Shower Enclosures

Bespoke glass shower cubicles built with 10mm toughened safety glass and high-grade anti-corrosive brass/SS designer hardware.

  • Hydrophobic Anti-Limescale Nano Coating
  • Rose Gold, Matte Black, & Chrome Fittings
  • Magnetic Water-Tight Edge Seals
  • Sliding & Swing Hinged Configurations

6. Structural Steel Trusses

Heavy structural steel sheds, tubular pipe roof trusses, industrial factory structures, and custom terrace pergolas.

  • ISMC Channel & Hollow Tubular Sections
  • High-Tension Bolted & MIG Welded Joints
  • Polycarbonate & Puff Panel Roofing
  • Anti-Rust Epoxy Primer Coating

7. Motorized & CNC Gates

Custom laser-cut CNC steel entrance gates fitted with automatic Italian sliding motors and smart remote access controls.

  • Heavy Duty Fiber Laser CNC Cut Designs
  • Italian Automatic Motor Drive Systems
  • Wrought Iron & Stainless Steel Combinations
  • Remote & Mobile App Access Control

8. Structural Glass Facades

Curtain wall glazing and spider fitting glass facades for commercial office buildings, showrooms, and modern retail hubs.

  • Stick Curtain Wall & Unitized Glazing
  • SS 316 Spider Fittings & Glass Fins
  • Low-E Energy Efficient Reflective Glass
  • Structural Silicone Weatherproofing

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions? We Have Answers

Everything you need to know about site measurements, warranties, and custom orders.

Which regions do you cover for fabrication and installation?
We actively service all major locations across Haryana, Punjab, Chandigarh, Mohali, Panchkula, and Himachal Pradesh. For large commercial projects, we deploy teams anywhere in North India.
What is the lead time from measurement to site delivery?
Standard UPVC and Aluminium window/door orders take 7 to 12 working days. Custom glass railings, ACP facade cladding, and heavy steel trusses take 10 to 18 days depending on site dimensions.
What warranties do you offer on UPVC and Alu Shield profiles?
Our UPVC profiles carry a 10-to-15 year guarantee against discolouration, warping, and UV damage. Alu Shield system aluminium profiles come with a 15-year coating warranty and 2-year hardware replacement warranty.
Can I request a site inspection before finalizing the quote?
Yes! Owner Suchit Kumar Kulshrestha or a senior team member will visit your site to record accurate laser measurements and evaluate structural framing requirements.
